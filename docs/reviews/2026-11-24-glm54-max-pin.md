## Threshold and method

The controller ran the original six IronAFS code ranges with the
`glm-5.4` max pin using the same frozen key and scope. The pass rule
required 6/6 critical and at least 9/12 planted defects, six complete
runs, zero false blockers and at most one false major. For the original
suite, one finding may credit one planted defect. The original and fresh keys
are identified by their digests in the high-effort record. Certifying completion
required the exact served model, explicit effort, structured verdict
and per-run end readback.

A separate paired max-effort comparison used the same three fresh
ranges reviewed at high. Its frozen key required 4/4 critical defects,
all three ranges complete, zero false blockers and at most one false
major, including the clean case. Both model pins completed every range.
A turn-capped run for each pin resumed in its own original session.
The totals include initial and resumed usage.
