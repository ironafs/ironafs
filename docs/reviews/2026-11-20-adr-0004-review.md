# Review record: ADR 0004, the architecture

The rounds that reviewed [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-architecture.md), each
finding and its disposition. The ADR's own Review section summarises them.
Lane output was treated as data: every finding was checked against the
text before it was acted on, and none was accepted or rejected by vote.

## Seats

The ADR was drafted and fixed by Claude models (Mythos 5.2 drafted the four
parts; Opus 5.5 reconciled them with the claim record and wrote the fixes).
The required seats are GLM (security and design) and GPT (design); GPT
reviewed earlier drafts of the design and wrote none of this text
([docs/workflow.md](../workflow.md) §2). Gemini took a design seat in round
1 only. A DeepSeek design seat was planned and not run.

| Seat | Served model | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| GLM | `glm-5.3` | max |
| GPT | `gpt-6.2-astra` | xhigh |
| Gemini (round 1) | `gemini-4-carbon-high` | high |

Every run passed its did-it-run checks (served model and effort read from
the lane's own log, completion, check code and end marker, real files).
One Gemini run (part 1, decisions C25 to C46) ended without output when its
quota ran out; it is void, and those decisions were reviewed by GLM and GPT.
