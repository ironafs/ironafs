### Round 1

All findings in this round were present in the initial design.

- **GLM F1 and F8, Gemini F1 and F2 (contract).** The initial old-header
  result would have weakened full ABI proof or left the report
  ambiguous. The design now requires complete proof for each implemented
  target, with a separately reported glibc 2.28 compatibility run and
  an explicit musl absence report.
- **GLM F2 and F4, Gemini F4 and F5 (correctness).** The fixture could
  disagree with the release build on effective codegen units, local or
  cross-crate LTO, incremental compilation or other compiler options.
  The design now captures real compiler argument vectors, probes the
  shipped binary's target compilation before linking, compares
  effective options and handles assembly shards.
- **GLM F3, F6 and F7, Gemini F3 (correctness and contract).** A
  missing include or feature macro, a filename or an unavailable
  declaration with a macro still present could masquerade as absence.
  The design requires a target-specific header baseline, an exact
  primary diagnostic, and a companion preprocessor probe. The only
  approved-only probe is row 14's `SCM_PIDFD` on the two musl targets.
- **GLM F5, Gemini F6 (contract).** The assertion omitted in a legacy
  partial run was underspecified. The design validates the original
  exact typed declaration and removes only that assertion from
  generation. An indirect use that cannot compile fails.

### Round 2

The second fix addressed all eleven reports from the scoped verification.

- **GLM F1 (pre-existing, contract), F2 (introduced by fix round 1,
  correctness), Gemini F2 (introduced by fix round 1, contract).**
  Missing evidence in the old-header job applies to the named assertion
  in that job. The native test exercises `SCM_RIGHTS` descriptor passing.
  The compatibility check fails for requalification if its headers
  supply the name.
- **GLM F3 and F5, Gemini F1 (introduced by fix round 1, correctness).**
  A configured wrapper, dependency failure or wrong target command
  could corrupt the captured evidence. The design pins the compiler and
  wrapper, verifies successful prerequisites and the distinctive
  pre-link stop, and checks the binary and its target dependencies.
- **GLM F4 (introduced by fix round 1, contract).** Each musl job must
  observe one withheld result and reject duplicate, missing or complete
  results for row 14.
- **GLM F6 (introduced by fix round 1, correctness).** An assembly
  alias follows its defining shard, with cycles and multiple or
  unresolved definitions refused.
- **Gemini F3 (pre-existing, correctness).** The baseline proves that
  GNU features were enabled before the first include using
  `sizeof(struct ucred)` and, on glibc, `__USE_GNU`.
- **Gemini F4 (introduced by fix round 1, correctness).** Accumulated
  target-feature arguments compare in order. Recognised singleton
  settings use the compiler's last setting.
- **Gemini F5 (pre-existing, contract).** Validate qualified names
  before branching on the probe, and prove their values by name.

### Round 3 and convergence

The rounds moved from two GLM blockers and one Gemini blocker to none
in both lanes. GLM's high findings went from three to two to none,
and Gemini's major findings from four to four to none. Round 3
introduced two wording reports and left no reported issue with the
wipe design. There are no confirmed open correctness or contract
findings. The two-fix limit was reached.

DeepSeek Flash max (uncalibrated on IronAFS) independently ruled on
GLM's four non-blocking reports using the entire frozen ABI design and
exact amendment, including lines outside GLM's scoped diff. The lane
verified the served model, nonce and brief marker. Quota headroom
was not reported.

- **GLM F1 (pre-existing, correctness).** The unchanged companion probe
  rejects a present but unusable macro under the same baseline. Confirmed
  with GCC and Clang. The reported gap does not hold.
- **GLM F2 (pre-existing, contract).** The validation applies to each
  qualified name. A row without a qualified name calls for no typed
  constant. The reported gap does not hold.
- **GLM F3 (introduced by fix round 2, contract).** The report belongs to
  `check-abi`. Existing CI reads that command's log, and `check-os`
  compares the Rust gate and inventory statuses. The reported gap does
  not hold.
- **GLM F4 (introduced by fix round 2, other).** The worksheet's
  descriptor-passing test is the executed `SCM_RIGHTS` test. The design
  also identifies the distinct kernel-delivered pidfd test. The wording
  accurately describes the tested behavior.

## Supersession sweep

The controlling statements are Sheet 14 and ADR 0005 points 5.3, 5.5
to 5.7, 6.7 and 7. The changed obligations concern the evidence for the
existing row. The sites.json table and Rust cfg still agree on where
the row is compiled. ADR 0004 C43 point 4 specifies daemon peer
identity, and L7 point 1 specifies handing off the FUSE descriptor.
Their obligations stand. The older #57 review records describe the
rules and measurements of the revisions they reviewed.

The files that consume the evidence are the ABI checker and fixtures,
the CLI and its command dispatch, the inventory documentation, the
row's cfg comment, and the modern and compatibility Linux CI reports.
The maintainers and contributors applying that sheet, and CI applying
the report, use the updated scope.

  the name and the C assertion proved its value.
