[ADR 0006](../adr/0006-rx-unprotected-packets.md), each finding and its
disposition. The ADR's own Review section summarises them. Lane output
was treated as data: every finding was checked against the text and
ADR 0004 before it was acted on, and none was accepted or rejected by
vote.

## Round 4: owner decisions applied

The owner decided open questions 1 and 2 on 2026-11-20 (option A: the
identity rule stays in a working point and decision 1, point 9, gates
the ABORT's effects; option B: service id and security index matched
only as test-cell evidence supports). They were applied: decision 1,
point 1 (the two fields leave the required match, a mismatch is a
diagnostic) and point 3's tuple; the Alternatives' last bullet; the
second "Revisit if" trigger; the questions became "Decided questions".

GPT (`gpt-6.2-sol`, xhigh) checked the batch of drafts that carried
owner decisions. Its one finding on this ADR (major): test 2 still
dropped a packet for any field mismatch. Fixed: test 2 tests each field
of the mandatory tuple and the call number separately; a differing
service id, and separately a differing security index, does not drop
the abort, which ends its call, and the diagnostic count rises by one.
GLM (`glm-5.3`, high, security) found both decisions applied as decided,
with no finding on this ADR.
