# Review record: the first-party DNS client for Linux and Unix builds

## The change

Class S. Issue #60 (plan id M2a-51).

- `docs/specs/dns-client.md`: the one DNS client of glibc and musl
  builds (ADR 0004, C33, point 3). The messages it sends and accepts
  (SRV, AFSDB, A and AAAA, with CNAME, SOA and OPT as they appear in
  replies), each cap a named constant; the decoder's checks in a fixed
  order, matching before anything is trusted; how the host's resolver
  configuration is read and a local resolver detected; the flat-list
  confirmation, the explicit servers and the scope rule for cells
  reachable only through a VPN; one query's transport, tries, timeouts
  and forgery defences; a lookup of a cell's volume location or
  protection servers and its outcomes; expiry, the positive cache, the
  negative cache of C12, point 3, and the failure hold-down; the
  arithmetic; a closed error set; golden vectors, round-trip properties,
  a reject corpus, fuzz targets and acceptance cases; open questions Q1
  to Q7.
- `docs/specs/README.md`: register of editions rows E3 to E28 (the DNS
  and IP addressing RFCs, the expired 0x20 Internet-Draft, and the
  resolv.conf, systemd-resolved and NetworkManager manual pages), and
  the index row.
- **Written by** `claude-code:claude-opus-5-5` (drafting, and the fixes
  of every round).

**This is the version from documents.** Every wire fact rests on
published RFCs, cited by section and labelled. Three things are not yet
backed by anything run:

- the observations of section 12.1 (that each supported distribution's
  local resolver writes a loopback-only resolver configuration, and
  whether each keeps a query's letter case). Neither blocks the
  specification: a failure of either only reduces discovery;
- the route answer that scoped cells need from the network-generation
  source (#97), marked MISSING; until it is specified, a scoped cell
  sends nothing (section 13, Q4);
- the driver's thread and the seam (section 13, Q1 and Q2, with #71),
  which block the driver, not the codec or the state machine.

## Findings

Some findings overlap: GLM 7 and Gemini 5 are one defect, and the two
lanes' round 2 findings on the negative cache and the consultation order
are one.

GLM, round 1, all fixed in `bce671d`:

- **Minor:** the reason given for `dns.tcp_reply_max` (16,384 bytes) was
  arithmetically false: 64 SRV records with 255-byte targets need about
  17,472 bytes. Fixed: the cap is kept and the reason now gives the true
  arithmetic; a larger answer is `MessageTooLarge`, a try failure, never
  a negative.
- **Minor:** the redraw of source-port draws below 1,024 had no bound.
  Fixed: a try makes at most 16 draws of either kind, then fails with
  `NoSourcePort`; A10 and T28 follow.
- **Minor:** the failure hold-down was set by `NetworkChanged`,
  `NoServer` and `WithheldByScope`, so a network change could leave a
  stale failure on the new network. Fixed: only a lookup that sent a try
  and failed while its generation pair was current sets one, and an
  entry dies with its generation pair; T35.
- **Minor:** section 3's summary of "not a cell" left out the "not
  offered" route. Fixed.
- **Minor:** `dns.addresses_max` had no rule enforcing it. Fixed: more
  applicable A or AAAA records than the cap make the reply `Unusable`;
  R42.
- **Minor:** T29 never filled the wait list, so `Busy` was never reached.
  Fixed: 8 running and 64 waiting, then one more.
- **Minor:** R20 counted 4 labels of 63 bytes as 256 bytes with the
  root; it is 257. Fixed with Gemini's fifth finding.

Gemini, round 1, all fixed in `bce671d`:

- **Major:** a cell scoped to the local resolver could have its name
  forwarded out of the default route while the VPN was down, against
  C33, point 5; and the targets' address queries did not say they keep
  the cell's scope. Fixed: the `local-resolver` scope is removed; a
  scoped cell uses only its own servers, with the route checked before
  every try, and every query of its lookups, the address queries
  included, takes the cell's switch, scope and path. Departure from the
  first suggestion (gate the local resolver on a route check): a route
  to the local resolver says nothing about where it forwards a name. A
  cell is known to be reachable only through a VPN only from its scope
  key; an unmarked cell is a documented residual, R4. T16 and T16a.
- **Major:** a protection-server lookup could fill the machine-wide
  negative cache, so a cell that publishes no protection-server records
  would be marked "not a cell". Fixed: lookups, caches, the hold-down and
  coalescing are keyed by cell and service; only a volume location
  lookup can give "Not a cell"; a protection-server lookup gives "No
  service", which is not cached; T31.
- **Major:** the positive cache was not specified and its sections
  disagreed; a lookup with no usable address could be cached as
  servers; CNAME records' TTLs were ignored. Fixed: 5.6.7, point 10
  specifies the cache; a lookup with no usable address ends `NoAddress`;
  one with some unresolved targets is cached for at most 30 seconds;
  the chain's CNAME TTLs count in the lowest TTL; R44, T32 and T36.
- **Major:** the size checks came before matching, so an off-path sender
  who guessed only the source port could end a UDP try with a short or
  oversized datagram, bypassing the move to TCP. Fixed: on UDP a
  datagram is matched before its size is judged, and one that cannot be
  matched is dropped and counted; a try without OPT is capped at 512
  bytes; a wrong cross-reference was corrected; R1, R2, R2a, R2b and
  T37.
- **Major:** the name-length check did not count the root's zero byte,
  so a name of 256 bytes in wire form passed. Fixed: the 255 bytes
  include every length byte and the final zero byte (RFC 1035, §3.1),
  and a label is accepted only if the zero byte still fits; A4, R20,
  R20a and R20b.
- **Major:** the refused addresses missed IPv4-mapped and
  IPv4-compatible IPv6 addresses and most of 0.0.0.0/8. Fixed: 5.4,
  point 9 lists the refused blocks from RFC 4291, RFC 6890 and RFC 5771,
  registered as E26 to E28; private and unique-local addresses stay
  accepted; R29 and R29a. Gemini's suggested citations were not used:
  RFC 5156 is obsoleted by RFC 6890.
- **Minor:** one caller's short deadline could end a shared lookup for
  every caller and set a hold-down. Fixed: a caller's deadline ends only
  its own wait; the lookup runs to its own deadline; T33.
- **Minor:** the RDATA rule was worded for names only, so it broke the
  SOA record; pointer targets in the header were allowed; an SOA
  MINIMUM with its top bit set was unhandled; several kept SOA records
  had no rule. Fixed: fields read through a cursor bounded by the RDATA
  end; `PointerIntoHeader`; MINIMUM read as a TTL is; the lowest of
  several lifetimes; R43, R45, R46 and R47.
- **Minor:** the EDNS fallback, repeated in the second round, could give
  one server four UDP tries; and the resolver configuration was not
  re-read before the caches were consulted. Fixed: once a server has
  needed the fallback, later tries of the query to it omit OPT; the view
  is re-read first; T34 and T36.

GLM, round 2, all fixed in `21df6b5`:

- **Minor:** the cache consultation order and its cross-reference
  disagreed. Fixed: the negative cache, then the positive cache, then
  the hold-down, cited in that order.
- **Minor:** a protection-server lookup that hit the negative cache had
  no defined outcome. Fixed with Gemini's first finding.
- **Minor:** the re-read before the caches did not say what happens when
  the resolver file disappears. Fixed: a path whose metadata cannot be
  read has the identity "missing", a change to or from it is a change,
  and the view is then none; T39.

Gemini, round 2, all fixed in `21df6b5`:

- **Minor:** a negative-cache hit's outcome for a protection-server
  lookup was undefined, and a longer-lived positive entry could outlive
  the cell's negative entry. Fixed: the negative cache is consulted
  first; a hit ends a volume location lookup "Not a cell" and a
  protection-server lookup "No service"; filling the negative cache drops
  the cell's positive entries for both services; T38.
- **Minor:** the negative cache was filled "with a lifetime", where R46
  expects a lifetime of 0 not to be cached; and a "not offered" answer
  reached through a CNAME took only the SRV record's TTL. Fixed: "a
  lifetime above 0"; a "not offered" lifetime counts the chain's CNAME
  TTLs; R48.

No finding was refuted or deferred.

## Open items

- "No service" for a protection-server lookup is not cached, so repeated
  lookups of a cell that publishes no protection servers query again each
  time, bounded by coalescing and the lookup deadline. GLM noted this as
  a choice, not a defect; it is the specification's own, and a short
  cache can be added if measurement asks for it.
- Section 13, Q1 to Q7, as written there.
