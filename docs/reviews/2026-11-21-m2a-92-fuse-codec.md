The new mappings come from [VFS contract §5.12](../specs/vfs-contract.md#512-errors-per-operating-system). The pinned E74 UAPI source provides their names and numbers. `Protocol` shares `EIO` with `NotYetStored`, and `PastEnd` maps to `ENXIO`. The spec distinguishes the generic E74 descriptions of `ENODATA` and `ERANGE` from the operation conditions in the VFS contract. The remaining per-operation syscall-page check at [codec §13 Q7](../specs/fuse-codec.md#13-open-questions) is unchanged.

The root manifest adds one sorted first-party workspace row, and the lockfile adds the sole `ironafs-fuse` entry. The workspace member rule and codec manifest are unchanged. VFS source and its contract remain identical to main. Of the 18 pinned codec files, only `crates/ironafs-fuse/src/errno.rs` and its tests differ. The codec spec changes only §5.7. Its index row and E70–E74 register entries are the six byte-identical carried additions.

The closed `Errno` set adds six positive values. The exhaustive `for_error` match covers all 29 `VfsError` variants. The existing rename, name and INIT exceptions retain their behavior. The existing reply encoder consumes the request token, negates the positive errno and writes only the reply header. The unchanged session binding gives out bytes after checking session ownership and retiring a pending request. This review concerns the offline codec. Native mounted-FUSE evidence has a separate gate in PR #195.
