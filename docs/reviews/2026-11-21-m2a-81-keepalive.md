# Review record: the keepalive and server time specification

## The change

Class S. Issue #81 (plan id M2a-64). This is the first version, written
from documents only; the issue stays open for the version that adds the
observation lab's and the test cell's facts
(keepalive-and-server-time.md, section 13, Q1).

- `docs/specs/keepalive-and-server-time.md`: the keepalive procedure, its
  connection, identity and caps; how callback state is established on
  the client; when keepalives are sent; the exact conditions under which
  a keepalive's reply raises callback trust, what a raise covers (the
  lowering count) and its end; failed and local outcomes; the K step
  that makes every read and change of trust one step over the server
  key's record; server time ("not measured" in M2a, and only able to
  shorten a promise once measured); the time fields; counters, status
  and tests.
- `docs/specs/callbacks.md`: the lowering count beside the fence tuple
  (not part of it), compared in the trusted branch of 5.6, point 5,
  raised at every lowering and at the end of a raised trust (including a
  network change); the K step; the failed and local outcome split in 5.6,
  point 11; test C25.
- `docs/specs/README.md`: the index row, register rows E75 (the AFS
  registry's file service procedure table), E76 (RFC 4787) and E77 (two
  OpenAFS reference pages), and this specification added to E11 and
  E12's "Cited by". E75 is the same page as the VL and FS read
  specification's row E5 and merges into it once both are on `main`.
- **Written by** `claude-code:claude-opus-5-5` (drafting, and the fixes of
  every round).
- **Merge order.** The specification rests on the callback specification
  (#66), the Rx specification (#55), ADR 0006 and the seams
  specification (#71), and on ADR 0004's amendment in #53, point 3; it
  merges after them and does not leave draft before #53 is accepted.

## Findings

Every finding is quoted verbatim, with its disposition, in the
controller's fix log for this change; the summary here is by reference.
None was refuted. One direction was adapted (round 1, the send stamp).

### Round 1

- **Major (GLM; DeepSeek minor):** a lowering could fall between the
  raise's check and its write. Fixed: the K step (keepalive 5.6, point
  10; callbacks.md 5.6, point 11), one step under the server key's record
  lock at the server level of ADR 0004 C39, point 4; test KA21. A
  compare-and-swap on the lowering count alone was not taken: the raise
  also depends on the lapse check and the fence values.
- **Major (DeepSeek):** a failed keepalive has no send stamp to count the
  next due time from. Fixed by `ka_from`: a reply's `first_sent` after a
  completed call, otherwise the arrival of the housekeeping step that
  handled the outcome. A stamp the keepalive owner takes itself was not
  used, because the seams specification's call-site check accepts only an
  arrival or a `first_sent` as a deadline's origin.
- **Major (DeepSeek):** local outcomes counted as server failures. Fixed:
  the failed and local outcome split (keepalive 5.6, point 8); test KA22.
- **Major (DeepSeek):** "server time feeds no deadline" against the
  shortening of a promise's lifetime. Fixed: it never sets or lengthens a
  deadline, and its one use on an expiry is the shortening.
- **Minors:** KA8 tested an unreachable path (now tested at the seam);
  the index row lacked ADR 0006 D1.9; early causes lacked an advance and a
  pause; RFC 4787 facts lacked verdict tags; the A14 bound's premise; the
  identity slot table; the connection's idle time; the VL and FS read
  specification's sentence on procedure 153 (a cross-PR item, below).
  All fixed.

### Round 2

- **Major (DeepSeek; GLM minor):** the network-change end of trust had no
  state to detect it. Fixed: trust records the network generation and
  episode count in force at the raise.
- **Minors:** the outcome split mirrored in callbacks.md 5.6, point 11; a
  completed reply without a send stamp; a failed open's slot and the
  no-slot skip's `ka_from`; the A14 wording against callbacks' reset rows;
  the idle time `Rpc::open` cannot take (moved to the #71 item). All
  fixed or moved.

### Round 3

- **Minor (DeepSeek):** the slot release was keyed to one error the seam
  does not carry. Fixed: any failed open frees its slot; the error is
  added to the #71 item.

Residuals the reviewers accepted as stated: an on-path attacker can keep
a server trusted while dropping its breaks (ADR 0004 C32, "Why"); a gap
counted from `first_sent` can be shorter than the true gap by the
hand-off margin; the connection layer's writes of `hold` are not K
steps.
